HOW TO CUSTOMIZE

Sync your theme using only your GitHub Username.

1. Fork the Repository

Go to the official repository at ://github.com and click the Fork button in the top right corner to create a copy under your account.

2. Edit Your Colors

Open the colors.txt file in your fork, click the edit pencil icon, and change the 7 lines to your preferred hex codes (Line 1 is Sunday, Line 7 is Saturday). Commit and save your changes.

3. Enter Your Username

  1. Return to Time24.cc and click Customize.
  2. Type in your exact GitHub Username (e.g., octocat).
  3. Press Save & Sync. Your browser handles the rest!
